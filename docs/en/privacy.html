Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 20, 2025

Government Affiliation Disclaimer

Buscyl Route Finder is a private initiative and is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing the Junta de Castilla y León or any other government entity.

The route data displayed in this application is obtained from publicly available Open Data provided by the regional government.

Official Data Source: Junta de Castilla y León Open Data

1. Introduction

Welcome to Buscyl Route Finder. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you use our application and tell you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

2. Data We Collect

Buscyl Route Finder uses analytics services to better understand how the application is used. We do not collect your name, email address, or other direct personal contact information.

Through Google Analytics, we may collect technical and usage data, including:

  • Device information (model, operating system version).
  • Usage data (features used, frequency of use).
  • Approximate location (city/region level, derived from IP address).
  • Crash and performance information (Crashlytics).

3. Use of Information

The technical information collected is used solely to:

  • Improve the functionality of the application.
  • Resolve technical errors.
  • Analyze usage trends in an aggregated manner.

4. Application Permissions

The application may request the following permissions:

  • Internet Access: Required to download and update bus schedules.
  • Storage: May be required to save local preferences or schedule caches.

5. Third-Party Services

The application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or analyze application usage.

Links to privacy policy of third-party service providers used by the app:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics

6. Data Retention

We retain technical data collected through Google Analytics for Firebase for up to 14 months. Crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics are retained for up to 90 days.

7. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us your information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. All data transmission is encrypted. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Since data is processed and stored by third-party services (Google Firebase), we do not have direct control over or access to personally identifiable data. Exercising your rights (access, deletion, rectification) must be done in accordance with the policies and tools provided by Google.

You can manage your privacy through your device settings (e.g., resetting your Advertising ID) or by consulting Google's Privacy Policy.

9. Data Deletion

Collected data is managed by Google Analytics and Firebase. To request data deletion, you must follow the mechanisms provided by Google or uninstall the application, which will stop any future data collection.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

11. Children's Privacy

Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
